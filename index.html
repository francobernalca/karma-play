Keep little hands in the game

iPhone and iPad: turn on Guided Access in Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access. Open Karma Play, then triple-click the side button (or the Home button) and tap Start.

Android: turn on App pinning in Settings, Security (on some phones: Security and privacy, More settings). Open Karma Play, open Recent apps, tap the app's icon and choose Pin.

Play offline from the home screen

  1. iPhone and iPad, Safari: tap Share (on iPhone with the compact tab bar, tap the three dots first), then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android, Chrome: open the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Open it once while online. After that every balloon, picture and voice clip works without a connection.

Privacy
